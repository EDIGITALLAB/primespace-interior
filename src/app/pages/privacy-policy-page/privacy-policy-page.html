1. Overview

Prime Space Interior ("Prime Space", "we", "our", or "us") operates the website and interior design consultation services. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you visit our website, submit consultation requests, or engage with our design studios.

By accessing or using our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our interior design services:

  • Personal Contact Information Full Name, Email Address, 10-digit Phone Number, Home Address, and Preferred Design Studio Location submitted via consultation forms.
  • Project & Floor Plan Details Floor plan drawings, room dimensions, budget preferences, layout choices, and material requirements provided for interior consultations.
  • Device & Usage Data IP address, browser type, operating system, page views, and interaction timestamps collected automatically via standard web cookies.

3. How We Use Your Data

Prime Space Interior uses the collected data for the following purposes:

Consultation Scheduling

To connect you with our lead interior architects and book physical/virtual studio walkthroughs.

Quotations & 3D Plans

To prepare custom 3D layouts, itemized cost estimates, and material sample boards tailored to your budget.

Customer Support

To update you on live site construction progress, factory manufacturing, and 10-year warranty claims.

Service Updates

To inform you about newly launched experience centers, package offers, or seasonal styling trends (only if opted in).

4. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your data is strictly kept confidential within Prime Space Interior.

We implement industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption and strict access controls across our internal design databases to ensure your personal data is protected against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

5. Cookies Policy

Cookies are small files placed on your device to help us analyze web traffic and customize your browsing experience. You can choose to accept or decline cookies through your browser settings. Declining cookies may limit certain interactive features of our website.

6. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, request corrections, or ask for the deletion of your data from our consultation records at any time by contacting our Privacy Team.

Our Data Protection Officer is available to address any privacy or data concerns.
