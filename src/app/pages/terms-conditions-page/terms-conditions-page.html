2. Scope of Design Services

Prime Space Interior provides turnkey interior design, factory-manufactured modular furniture, 3D VR space planning, electrical/plumbing conduit routing, and site installation.

  • All 3D renders are artistic representations. Final factory outputs match 1:1 with approved 2D production drawings.
  • Any structural changes (beam cutting, load-bearing walls) require client approval from certified civil engineers.
  • Custom material choices (natural veneers, marble slabs) may vary slightly in grain pattern due to natural characteristics.

4. Project Timelines & Site Readiness

Our standard move-in commitment is 45 Days from factory order sign-off. Timelines are subject to site readiness, uninterrupted power/water supply, and builder possession handover. Delays caused by third-party civil contractors or delayed client approvals will extend the delivery schedule accordingly.

5. 10-Year Structural Warranty Terms

Prime Space Interior provides a 10-year structural warranty against manufacturing defects on all BWP marine plywood carcasses and factory modular joinery.

Warranty Exclusions: Normal wear and tear, intentional physical damage, water seepage from external builder plumbing leaks, and modifications executed by third-party technicians not authorized by Prime Space.
